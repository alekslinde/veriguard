Veriguard is installed

Checks run on your device. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

1 Pin it to your toolbar

Open your browser's extensions menu — the puzzle-piece or jigsaw icon near the address bar — and pin Veriguard. That toolbar button is where results appear, so pinning it is the difference between seeing a verdict and going looking for one.

2 Check something you've received

Select the suspicious text on any page — an email, a message, a link, a phone number — then right-click it and choose Check this with Veriguard.

You can also click the toolbar button at any time and paste something in directly.

3 Read the result

The toolbar button shows a badge as soon as the check finishes, and you'll get a notification with the verdict. Click the button to see the full breakdown — every signal that was found, and what it scored.

Veriguard also tells you what it couldn't check. A quiet result with a coverage notice means less than a quiet result without one, and the panel says so rather than leaving you to assume.
