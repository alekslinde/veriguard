Veriguard is installed

Three things to know. Checks run on your device — nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

  1. Open your browser's extensions menu — the puzzle-piece or jigsaw icon near the address bar — and pin Veriguard. That toolbar button is where results appear, so pinning it is the difference between seeing a verdict and going looking for one.

  2. Select the suspicious text on any page — an email, a message, a link, a phone number — then right-click it and choose Check this with Veriguard. You can also click the toolbar button at any time and paste something in directly.

  3. The toolbar button shows a badge as soon as the check finishes, and you'll get a notification with the verdict. Click the button for the full breakdown — every signal found, and what it scored.

Veriguard also tells you what it couldn't check. A quiet result carrying a coverage notice means less than a quiet one without; the panel says so rather than leaving you to assume. It is rule-based, so a brand-new scam that matches nothing will pass — treat anything that asks for money or details with suspicion regardless of the verdict.

Veriguard is rule-based — keyword patterns, domain lists and weighted scoring, all running locally. It has no AI model and makes no call to an analysis service. It will miss new scams that don't match its rules, which is why every verdict tells you what it checked.
